Some FAQs

1. How do I create an account?
Creating an account is simple. Click on the Sign Up button, enter your basic details such as your name, email address, phone number, and password, then verify your account to start shopping.
2. What payment methods are available?
SmartBasket supports multiple secure payment options, including Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking, Digital Wallets, and Cash on Delivery (where available).
3.How can I track my order?
Once your order is confirmed, you can track its status by visiting the My Orders section in your account. You will also receive updates via email or SMS.
4. Can I cancel or return my order?
Yes, you can cancel your order before it is shipped. If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, you can request a return or replacement according to our Return & Refund Policy.
5. How long does delivery take?
Delivery time depends on your location and product availability. Most orders are delivered within 24–48 hours, while some locations may require additional time.
6. How can I contact SmartBasket customer support?
You can reach our customer support team through the Contact Us page, email, or phone. Our support team is available to assist you with orders, payments, returns, and any other queries.
